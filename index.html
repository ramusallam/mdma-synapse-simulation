Notes

Key Takeaways

  • MDMA powerfully increases serotonin in the synapse.
  • The brain compensates over time, leading to tolerance.
  • When the drug is removed, serotonin systems take time to recover, causing withdrawal.
  • Frequent or high use increases the risk of lasting problems with mood, memory, and emotional regulation.

Long-Term Risks

  • Changes in mood and emotional regulation
  • Memory and learning problems
  • Sleep disturbances
  • Possible long-term damage to serotonin systems
  • Increased risk of anxiety and depression

Important to Know

  • Effects vary with dose, frequency, environment, genetics, and mental health.
  • Hydration, avoiding overheating, and spacing uses can reduce (but not eliminate) risks.
  • If you or someone you know is struggling, help is available.
